Spending

Paid generation is off on a fresh install and stays off until you turn it on here. Even then, nothing is charged until a monthly cap above $0 is set and the specific account is authorised on Credits. Turning paid generation off again withdraws every account authorisation, so switching it back on later cannot quietly re-arm spending.

The cap counts a job's confirmed charge where the provider reported one, and its estimated cost otherwise. Estimates come from published rates and can be wrong, so the cap can hold back a job that would have been slightly cheaper - which is the safer direction. The ceiling this app accepts is {{ max_cap_usd | usd }} per month.

Generation defaults and queue behaviour

Defaults are applied when a job is queued. Each provider snaps them to the nearest setting its model accepts and records exactly what changed on the job.

The target is what Daily Capacity measures against. It is a goal, not a promise - the free allowances available do not reach 20 minutes a day, and that screen says by how much.

Attempts never apply to a rejected key, an empty balance, refused content or an uncertain outcome - retrying those either cannot help or risks paying twice. A provider's own minimum polling interval always wins over a shorter one here.
