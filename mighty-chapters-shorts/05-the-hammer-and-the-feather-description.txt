TITLE
A Hammer and a Feather Fell Together on the Moon

DESCRIPTION
On August 2, 1971, near the end of Apollo 15's last moonwalk, Commander David Scott dropped a geology hammer and a falcon feather. With no air to slow the feather, they landed together. Galileo had argued, centuries earlier, that without air the heavier object does not win.

Scott's words were, "How about that." The feather is still on the Moon.

Illustrated. These pictures are original art for Mighty Chapters, not the NASA film of the drop. The real footage is in the Apollo 15 record.
