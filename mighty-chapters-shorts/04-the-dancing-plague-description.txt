TITLE
The City That Could Not Stop Dancing

DESCRIPTION
In July 1518, in Strasbourg, a woman the chronicles call Frau Troffea began to dance and could not stop. Dozens joined her, then hundreds. Doctors blamed overheated blood, and the city built a stage and hired musicians, believing more dancing was the cure.

Contemporary writers say some of the dancers collapsed and died. Modern historians do not agree on a number, or on a single cause. Ergot in the rye is one idea. A fear that spread through a starving city is another.
