TITLE
Lions Rose Out of the Colosseum Floor

DESCRIPTION
Lions did not walk out of a gate. Under the Colosseum, lifts brought animals up through trapdoors in a wooden floor covered with sand. Sand, in Latin, is harena. That is where the word arena comes from.

The basement was added under the emperor Domitian. Ancient writers, including Cassius Dio, describe a naval show at the opening games, before that basement made flooding the floor impossible.
