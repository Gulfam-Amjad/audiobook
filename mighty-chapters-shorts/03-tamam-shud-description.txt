TITLE
The Dead Man and the Two-Word Code

DESCRIPTION
On December 1, 1948, a man was found dead on Somerton Beach in Australia. The labels were cut out of his clothes. A hidden pocket held a scrap torn from the Rubaiyat, a book of Persian poems. The words Tamam Shud mean "it is ended."

Another copy of the book held a phone number and a code that has never been broken. In 2022, DNA research pointed to an electrical engineer named Carl Webb. The code is still unread.

Illustrated. These pictures are original art for Mighty Chapters, not crime-scene photographs.
