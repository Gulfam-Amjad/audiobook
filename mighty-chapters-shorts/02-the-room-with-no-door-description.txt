TITLE
A Room in the Great Pyramid No One Has Entered

DESCRIPTION
In 2017 a team called ScanPyramids found a void at least 30 meters long above the Grand Gallery inside Khufu's pyramid. They did not drill. Cosmic particles called muons passed through the stone, and the journal Nature published the result.

In 2023 a camera saw a smaller corridor behind the north face. That corridor is not the big void. The big void still has no door.

Illustrated. These pictures are original art for Mighty Chapters, not footage from inside the pyramid.

Every chapter holds a story.
