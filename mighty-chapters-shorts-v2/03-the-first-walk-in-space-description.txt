TITLE
He Walked in Space First, Then Died in a Fire

DESCRIPTION
On June 3, 1965, Ed White became the first American to walk in space. He was tethered to Gemini 4 and moved with a handheld gun that fired bursts of oxygen. After about twenty-one minutes, mission control called him back in. On January 27, 1967, White died in a fire inside the Apollo 1 capsule during a launch rehearsal, along with Gus Grissom and Roger Chaffee.
