TITLE
The Astronaut Who Almost Drowned in Space

DESCRIPTION
On July 16, 2013, Italian astronaut Luca Parmitano was outside the International Space Station, about 250 miles up. Water from his suit's cooling system leaked and drifted into his helmet. He could not see, and water was close to his face. His partner Chris Cassidy helped guide him back to the airlock, and NASA called off the spacewalk to investigate the leak.
